# Museums

A list of all museums I have visited and/or plan to visit. 
Each museum is rated 1-5 stars alongside a brief description of the museum itself and my thoughts. 
There are three (semi-overlapping) criteria I’ll be evaluating for each museum:

* **Education.** Is it interesting? Learning was my first love and it will be my last. A museum should be detailed with its subject matter. This is a delicate balance between diligence and accessibility. If I am visiting a museum on American imperialism in Afghanistan, I want it to discuss how Zbigniew Brzezinski instigated the initial Soviet invasion in 1979. It would be an academic (and moral) failure to begin the timeline at 9/11. At the same time, if I am uneducated on a subject, I want its introduction to be cohesive. There is a saying in mathematical pedagogy that an instructor should "tell the truth, nothing but the truth, but never the whole truth." That is, there will always be more detail. Your obsession with detail should not transform into a madness. 
* **Presentation.** Is it pretty? A detailed encyclopedia will always be more descriptive and educational than any museum. However, museums are not solely educational. Presentation matters! (This is closely related to accessibility). In the Landesmuseum in Zürich, the history exhibit has the visitor stroll down a physical timeline that illustrates the events of Switzerland’s history. The presentation assists the user in retaining the material being displayed.
* **Fun.** Was it fun? An experience can be both enlightening and beautiful, but I ultimately do not care if it was not enjoyable. I would not suggest a friend watch a 16 hour, 4k, 120 fps documentary on strawberries (although I would likely watch it myself), as the process itself is exhausting. Museum fatigue is real. After 2 hours meandering through any museum, I will be exhausted. However, the degree to which my exhaustion settles depends on the experience itself. A well-crafted experience should keep its visitors invigorated throughout the entire escapade.
